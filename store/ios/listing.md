## English

**Name** (30):
FISH ID: Species & Freshness

**Subtitle** (30):
Red Sea & Arabian Gulf fish

**Promotional text** (170):
Snap a fish at the market to see its species and local name, a freshness score out of 100, and how long it will last. 447 Red Sea and Gulf species.

**Keywords** (100 bytes):
fish,seafood,grouper,fresh,market,fishing,species,identify,Red Sea,Gulf,Saudi,KSAFish

**Description:**

FISH ID helps you buy fish with confidence. Take a photo of a fish at the market or on the boat, and the app tells you its species and local name, estimates how fresh it is, and shows you everything about it.

Why FISH ID?
• Knows Red Sea and Arabian Gulf fish by their local names, from the KSAFish encyclopedia of 447 species.
• Shows up to 3 likely species with a confidence score, so you can tell look-alikes apart, such as groupers, emperors and snappers.
• Scores freshness with the scientific Quality Index Method (QIM): eyes, gills, skin and texture, giving a score out of 100 and an estimate of days since catch.
• Add a close-up of the eye and a photo of the gills for a more accurate estimate.
• The "How long will it last?" calculator tells you how long the fish keeps based on how it was stored, and warns you about heat and about histamine fish such as kingfish, tuna and sardines.
• A full encyclopedia you can browse offline: family, local names, facts and sources.

Help build Saudi fish science
With your consent, you can send your photo with the species and days since catch, to help build the first dataset of fish sold in Saudi markets. We remove location and device data from the photo before saving it.

Subscription
Your first 3 scans are free. After that, you can subscribe monthly or yearly inside the app. Subscriptions renew automatically, and you can cancel any time in your Apple account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the period. The encyclopedia and the shelf-life calculator are always free.
